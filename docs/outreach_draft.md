# Outreach draft: one feedback request to an analog-campaign team

To be sent by a team member (not by an agent), after judging, to one person who plans analog field
tests (for example a NASA JETT or Desert RATS planner, the ESA PANGAEA team, or the CSA analog
program). Ask for one concrete thing: would a campaign card change how they pick a site?

Subject: Which test results transfer from an Earth analog site? A 2-minute look

Dear <name>,

We are a two-person team from NASA Space Apps 2026. We built SIMILITUDE, an open tool that judges
Earth analog sites per test variable rather than per site: for a Moon Base region it says which
variables a field site reproduces (slope, simulated routes, low-Sun windows), which need a named
facility (gravity offload, thermal vacuum, dust), and which orbit cannot judge (rocks under 60 m).

One example: for Connecting Ridge, two sites outside known analog areas, in Argentina and on Baffin
Island, together cover the five field rows orbit can judge; the best classic site covers one. Our
slope from orbit matches the tilt Perseverance measured at 708 stops (rho = 0.80). Every test was
registered before it ran, and we publish the ones that failed.
