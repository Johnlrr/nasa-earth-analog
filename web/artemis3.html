Artemis III candidate regions · registered and run 15 Nov 2026

Where on Earth to rehearse each Artemis III region

NASA has named nine candidate landing regions for Artemis III. Five of them are mapped by NASA's 5 m lunar elevation models under the same name. For each of the five, SIMILITUDE lists the Earth sites in open countries whose slopes and simulated traverses match the region, and the smallest campaign that covers terrain and low-Sun tests.

The other four regions

    And a crewed landing on Mars

    How it is tested: for each region the reference is the most typical 4 km window of NASA's PGDA Product 78 elevation model; thresholds come from the region's own other windows (the rule pre-registered for the route test, V2). An Earth footprint matches when both its slope distribution and its simulated traverses (30 m planner) fall within the region's thresholds, on Copernicus GLO-30 only; a second elevation model is not checked here. Random land = 1,000 pre-registered random bare-land cells. Low-Sun days come from a geometric Sun model (every daylight minute below 7°). Terrain shadow and 5 m slopes are not checked for these regions. Pre-registration: config/prereg_artemis3.yaml; results: validation report §7g–7h.

    Data: NASA PGDA Product 78 (LRO LOLA, Barker et al. 2021), HiRISE DTM (NASA/JPL/University of Arizona), Copernicus DEM GLO-30, ERA5-Land, MODIS, TerraClimate, ESA WorldCover. Region names: NASA (28 Oct 2024). Code Apache-2.0.
