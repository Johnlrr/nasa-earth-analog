1 · The problem

The sites we rehearse on are too flat for the Moon Base

NASA's 2024 Civil Space Shortfall list includes Autonomous Surface Mobility and Navigation (ESDMD #0805) and Lunar Precision Landing and Hazard Avoidance for Human Exploration (ESDMD #1101). Both are rehearsed on Earth, on terrain chosen by fame and access.

What it means for a test: about 86 km of traverse at the median classic site meets as much terrain steeper than 8° as 10 km on the ridge (if the share along the path equals the areal share). Slopes are computed on a 30 m baseline on both worlds. NASA's limits (<10° for landing over 100 m, <20° for EVA over 2 km, NTRS 20250009323) are operational limits, so this compares Earth with the Moon, not with NASA's maps.

3 · What a field test there can and cannot show

A terrain match reproduces the slopes, not the Moon

Protocol for a slope-traverse and touchdown-terrain rehearsal at a matching site: test rover mobility and path planning on slopes, EVA traverse planning and hazard-map products; measure traverse energy, slip and path length per km in the slope classes <8°, 8–20° and >20°, and compare with predictions made on the lunar DEM.

      Data & limits
      • EMIT covers only about ±52° latitude (ISS orbit), so the spectral lens cannot see polar sites.
      • Earth's atmosphere hides the 1.4 and 1.9 µm water bands where CRISM is most sensitive, so hydration parameters are excluded on both worlds.
      • MICA spectra are Mars I/F (numerator) and EMIT is surface reflectance; they are compared only through band-depth parameters and continuum-removed shapes.
      • X1 is semi-quantitative: a configuration match, and Earth ice sits near its melting point.
      • Sigma is relative to the variation among Mars or Moon target sites (the population) and is shown for reading; ranking uses the raw distance d.
      • Percentile is computed against a random null sample; its size n is printed on the card.
      • Attention = OpenAlex count of analog papers naming the place under the B8 query; a site is called hidden only when that count is known, at most 10, and no cell lies in a known-analog zone.
      • Verdicts TRANSFERS, NO-ANALOG and UNOBSERVED are read from the pipeline's verdict table, never computed in this page.
